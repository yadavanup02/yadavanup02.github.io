About

Studying the reactions that shape the cosmos.

I am an experimental nuclear physicist specialising in nuclear astrophysics. My doctoral research at Technische Universität Dresden and Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf (HZDR) investigated the 14N(α,γ)18F reaction using the Felsenkeller underground accelerator laboratory and a new gas-jet target system.

Developing a decay station at the EMMA focal plane and preparing for transfer and fusion-evaporation reaction experiments using EMMA and TIGRESS at TRIUMF.

Research

Current and recent work

Radioactive-beam experiments

Integration and commissioning of the EMMA mass separator and TIGRESS gamma-ray spectrometer at TRIUMF, including development of a decay station at the EMMA focal plane.

Measurement of 14N(α, γ)18F reaction

Experimental study of low-energy resonances and their importance for stellar reaction rates, using the Felsenkeller underground laboratory.

A. Yadav et al. PhD Thesis, Technische Universität Dresden / HZDR

Gas-target systems

Development, characterisation, and online interferometric thickness measurement of gas-jet and extended gas targets for nuclear-astrophysics experiments.

A. Yadav et al. Nuclear Instruments and Methods in Physics Research A 1082, 171034 (2026).

Methods and facilities

How I do research

Accelerator experiments

Experimental nuclear astrophysics using ion beams, gas targets, underground accelerators, and radioactive-ion-beam facilities.

Detector systems

Gamma-ray spectroscopy with HPGe detector arrays, including TIGRESS; charged-particle and recoil detection; data acquisition with digitizer-based systems and MIDAS.

Target development

Design, commissioning, and characterisation of gas-jet targets, high-vacuum systems, and Mach-Zehnder interferometry for online target-thickness measurements.

Analysis and simulation

Experimental-data analysis and visualisation with C++, ROOT, and Python; Monte Carlo uncertainty calculations; GEANT4, SRIM, LISE++, and ANSYS Fluent simulations.
