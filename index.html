About

Studying the reactions that shape the cosmos.

I am an experimental nuclear physicist specialising in nuclear astrophysics. My doctoral research at Technische Universität Dresden and Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf investigated the 14N(α,γ)18F reaction using the Felsenkeller underground accelerator laboratory and a new gas-jet target system.

I am now a postdoctoral researcher at TRIUMF, contributing to the integration of the EMMA mass separator and TIGRESS gamma-ray spectrometer for radioactive-beam experiments. I am particularly interested in precision measurements, gas targets, detector systems, and the tools needed to turn experimental data into reliable reaction rates.

Research

Current and recent work

01

14N(α,γ)18F

Experimental study of low-energy resonances and their importance for stellar reaction rates, using the Felsenkeller underground laboratory.

02

Gas-target systems

Development, characterisation, and online interferometric thickness measurement of gas-jet and extended gas targets for nuclear-astrophysics experiments.

03

Radioactive-beam experiments

Integration and commissioning of the EMMA and TIGRESS systems at TRIUMF, including development of a decay station at the EMMA focal plane.

Open research

Software and analysis

I use C++, ROOT, and Python to analyse and visualise experimental nuclear-physics data. My public research-code repository contains organised analysis macros and thermonuclear reaction-rate tools related to the 14N(α,γ)18F project.
